# My Work and Artwork page preview

I have added two more pages to the preview: My Work and Artwork. You can open both from the top menu or the footer. The home page still has all its existing sections, and its work and gallery buttons now lead to these pages.

## My Work

My Work brings the stories, artwork and projects together in one place. It opens with the heading “Different worlds. One imagination.” The large heading, gold accents and decorative symbol follow the same anime-inspired style as the home page.

Visitors can choose All work, Books, Artwork or Projects. Each category shows how many items it contains. There is also a search box to find an item by words in its title or description. The result count changes as visitors filter or search. If nothing matches, a clear message and Reset filters button help them start again.

Each card shows a visual, a category label, a title and a short description. The first story gets a larger featured card in the full collection. The grid adjusts to smaller screens so the content stays easy to read on a phone.

The cards are previews at this stage. They do not open individual stories, chapters or project pages yet. Sample entries are labelled, and the final content can replace them later.

## Artwork

Artwork gives the illustrations their own gallery. It opens with “Some worlds need no words.” The layout puts the images first, with each artwork’s category, title and description underneath.

The current gallery includes “The golden hour” and “The midnight gate” as sample illustrations. Visitors can filter by the available categories or use the search box to find an artwork by its title or description. A result count shows how many series match. When there are no matches, visitors can reset the filters.

The cards have space between them so each image feels separate. The gallery adapts to smaller screens and uses the same rounded shapes, colours and typography as the rest of the website. Individual artwork detail pages are still paused, so the cards remain on the gallery page.

At the bottom, the invitation to connect takes visitors to the social links in the footer.

## Shared design

Both pages keep the dark and light theme options. Dark mode uses black and dark grey backgrounds, white text and yellow, gold and orange accents. Light mode uses a warm off-white background, dark text and deeper gold accents. The aim is to keep both appearances connected to the home page.

The sword cursor and short trail are available for mouse users. The page loader appears when moving between pages, while changing search or category filters keeps visitors in place without replaying it. The navigation, social links and footer stay consistent across all three visible pages.

This release is a design preview using local content. The search and filters work within that content; separate detail pages and the remaining pages are not part of this review yet.
